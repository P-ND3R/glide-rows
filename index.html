Glide Rows

Each row is one note. Drag its knob left for lower, right for higher, anywhere between C3 and C6. Press play and the red line sweeps across, sounding every knob it passes.

Line at C3

Space plays and stops. With a knob focused, ← → move it a semitone; hold Shift for 10-cent steps. Ping-pong sends the line back right to left, so the return trip plays the notes falling.
